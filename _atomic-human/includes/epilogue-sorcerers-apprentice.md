\subsection{Like the Sorcerer's Apprentice}

\figure{\includepng{\diagramsDir/atomic-human/Atomic_H_Epilogue-detail-sorcerers-apprentice}{55%}}{Like the sorcerer's apprentice, we have technology companies deploying software systems that cannot be controlled by their creators. Detail from Dan Andrews' Epilogue illustration for *The Atomic Human* @Lawrence-atomic24.}{epilogue-sorcerers-apprentice}

\notes{This panel from Dan Andrews' Epilogue drawing restates the sorcerer's apprentice metaphor with contemporary logos: server-racks march as enchanted brooms while the apprentice stares at a laptop. The point is not that machines become conscious. It is that creators and institutions can lose practical control of systems they have already released.}
