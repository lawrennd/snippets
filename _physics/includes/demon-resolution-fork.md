\subsection{Where Can the Entropy Sit?}

\slides{The two ledgers do not close under a Maxwellian gate. That is the fork, not the resolution.}

\slidesincremental{
* Illegal coarse graining: Maxwellian all the way to $v_\star$ --- both ledgers cannot close
* Physical trapdoor: the door thermalises [@Smoluchowski-gultigkeitsgrenzen14]
* Memoryful policy: the record fills; erasure is on the ledger [@Szilard-intelligenter29; @Landauer-irreversibility61]
* One ensemble for the whole box: sorting is a fluctuation, not a cycle [@Maxwell-theory71]
}

\speakernotes{Pointer only. Do not open Szilard, Landauer, or Ellis here. Name the families so the next worksheet has somewhere to go. Maxwell's own point is already on the quote slide: the second law is about bodies we deal with in large masses.}

\notes{Once the compartments are drawn, the demon is no longer a single puzzle. It is a choice of which coarse graining is legal, and therefore of where the missing entropy is allowed to sit.

If we keep Maxwellians all the way to the gate, particle-flux balance and energy-flux balance cannot hold together. The calculation that produced $T_L\approx 1.9$, $T_R\approx 23$ already used that illegal extension: it closed only the number ledger. The first resolution is to refuse the extension. The kinetic layers are not groups of the same kind as the bulks; their entropy is not $H(\pi_i)$ at a local $T$.

If the gate is a physical trapdoor, the door is a degree of freedom in the same bath. It jitters, it fails to stay open for the tail and shut for the body, and the rectification dies [@Smoluchowski-gultigkeitsgrenzen14]. Feynman's pawl is the same object.

If the gate is a policy that remembers who may pass, the record is on the ledger. Szilard isolates one bit of that record; Landauer prices erasing it [@Szilard-intelligenter29; @Landauer-irreversibility61; @Bennett-thermodynamics82].

If we refuse compartments altogether and write one equilibrium ensemble for the closed box, the sorting we see on the canvas is a fluctuation. Maxwell already said the second law need not apply to a being who handles individual molecules [@Maxwell-theory71].

Those are different books, not competing slogans. The next worksheet is to coarse-grain Maxwell: which of those descriptions you are allowed to write for each compartment, and what entropy each description may claim.}
