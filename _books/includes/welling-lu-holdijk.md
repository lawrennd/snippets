\notes{\subsection{Welling, Lu and Holdijk}}

\notes{Welling, Lu and Holdijk's *Generative AI and Stochastic Thermodynamics* (GAIST, [@Welling-generative26]) is a supplementary text for this module, not a required one. The book asks whether generative models and nonequilibrium thermodynamics are the same mathematics. Its answer is that they are: variational free energy is the ELBO, inference is a system relaxing (heat), and learning is work done on the Hamiltonian. Entropy is missing information in Jaynes' sense. The closing slogan is that free energy is all you need.}

\notes{That is a different question from ours. We ask whether superintelligence is a perpetual-motion claim, and we treat entropy as a no-go. Use the book for the physics of free energy, for the ELBO identification, and for the Wasserstein and Schrödinger-bridge geometries. Do not use it as a substitute for Crooks (2007), Amari, or the three-perspective comparison in week 4. A draft is on the course site.}
