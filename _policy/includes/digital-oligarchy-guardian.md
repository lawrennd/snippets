\notes{Eleven years ago I wrote in the *Guardian* that we should worry less about the rise of the machines than about a digital oligarchy: concentrating the power that comes with data in few hands. Panel 1 has set out what that concentration costs. The AI wave has not invented the problem. It has thickened it. Concentration still stops value spreading. That is the thread from that piece to today.}
