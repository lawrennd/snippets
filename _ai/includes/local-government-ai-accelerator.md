\subsection{Local Government AI Accelerator}

\slidesincremental{
* ai@cam programme with local authorities
* Councils as partners from the outset
* Proof-of-concept AI for real operational needs
* Built on public dialogue about AI in local government
}

\notes{The [Local Government AI Accelerator](https://www.ai.cam.ac.uk/calls/local-government-ai-accelerator/) is an ai@cam programme that pairs University of Cambridge researchers with local councils to develop practical, proof-of-concept AI solutions to real operational challenges. Funded by the Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government, it embeds councils as active partners from the outset rather than treating them as end-users of vendor products.}

\notes{Projects include planning map automation, social housing maintenance, housing surveys, fly-tipping detection, and support for vulnerable tenants. The programme also places public concerns into the research process, building on ai@cam's public dialogues on AI in local government.}

\notes{See [Cambridge launches AI Accelerator to help local government deploy AI](https://www.ai.cam.ac.uk/news/cambridge-launches-ai-accelerator-to-help-local-government-deploy-ai/).}
