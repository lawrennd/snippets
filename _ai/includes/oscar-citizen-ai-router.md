\subsection{Example: OSCAR}


\slidesincremental{
* [OSCAR](https://www.local.gov.uk/case-studies/oscar-orchestrated-service-citizen-ai-router): Orchestrated Service & Citizen AI Router
* Peterborough City Council — led by Julian Patmore (Digital & Customer Access)
* Governance stays central; specialist tools stay local; staff keep the judgment
}

\notes{OSCAR (Orchestrated Service & Citizen AI Router) is a vendor-agnostic
framework from Peterborough City Council for a single digital front door
across local government services. Julian Patmore and colleagues are building
it as a real-world harness, not a frontier-model demo. Councils have been
adopting AI in silos — parking bots, fault reporting, social prescribing —
which fragments the resident experience and duplicates governance. OSCAR
separates orchestration from specialist capability: it routes requests to
the right service agent under shared guardrails, PII redaction, permissions
and audit, while leaving high-stakes judgment with council staff.}

\notes{The platform is designed to run across web, WhatsApp and voice, and to
sit on existing systems rather than replace them. It is still early —
proof of concept, with a roadmap from librarian (FAQ / RAG) through
specialist tools to a true concierge that joins up transactions — but the
pattern is the one we want: role-holders close to the work specifying and
monitoring the system. See the [LGA case
study](https://www.local.gov.uk/case-studies/oscar-orchestrated-service-citizen-ai-router).}
