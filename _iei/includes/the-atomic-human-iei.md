\notes{What has that got to do with information theory? Key among those limitations is one we are not going to treat in this course --- I'd suggest death remains the domain of theology --- but it is true: we are all going to die. The difference this course will focus on is what I think of as the embodiment factor: my ability to communicate versus my ability to compute, and yours as well.

That limitation is one of the dominant reasons our intelligence is the way it is. Instead of dumping the contents of a computation, I have to stand here and say: it is a bit like Boltzmann did this; here is another story of Gibbs; this person took his own life. I have to turn these things into things you might be able to relate to, in order to communicate very complex subjects. You might disagree about how well they worked today.

You do not lose marks for believing in AGI, superintelligence, and existential catastrophe. I think the claim is empty. The argument we will use in this course is Shannon's bits.}
